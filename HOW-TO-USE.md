# Remod Flow: How to Use

_Updated September 29, 2026. This guide updates itself when you change your boxes._

## ☀️ Every morning (5 minutes)

1. Double-click **RemodFlow** on your desktop.
   - 💡 It opens in your web browser. Nothing to log in to.
2. Click **🔔 To-Do**. If there's a number on it, send everything on that page.
   - 💡 Those are check-in texts and monthly emails that are due today.
3. Click **🏠 My Leads** and look at the ✅ Pre-Qualified box. Text every card that says **Text them now!**
   - 💡 These are your best customers. Speed wins the job.

## 📥 When a new lead comes in

4. Open the Facebook lead email (or text) and copy all of it.
   - 💡 You don't have to clean it up. Messy is fine.
5. Click **➕ Add a Lead**, paste it in the big box, and press **✨ Add this lead**.
   - 💡 Remod Flow reads it and drops the customer in the right box by itself.
6. If they landed in ✅ Pre-Qualified: press **📋 Copy** under First text, send it from your phone, then press **✅ I sent it**. Try to do this within 5 minutes.
   - 💡 Pressing I sent it starts a 4-day timer for the check-in text.
7. If they landed in 📥 New Lead: call or text them to get the missing answers, then fix their info and press **💾 Save and re-sort**.
   - 💡 The green box tells you exactly what's missing.
8. If they landed in 🔄 Nurture/Follow-Up: do nothing now.
   - 💡 They aren't ready yet. Their monthly emails will show up on your To-Do page.

## 🚚 Moving a customer toward a signed job

Your boxes go in this order. Open a customer, do the step, then press the green **Next step** button.

9. **📥 New Lead:** Call or text them and ask 3 things: Do you own the home? What's your budget? When do you want to start? Open the customer, press ✏️ Change their info, type the answers, then press 💾 Save and re-sort.
   - 💡 Remod Flow can only sort a customer when it knows those 3 answers.
10. **👀 Pre-Sales Video Viewed:** They watched your pricing video. Ask their budget and start date, then press Next step.
   - 💡 People who watched the video already know your prices, so they're warmer.
11. **✅ Pre-Qualified:** Send the 💬 First text right away (📋 Copy, paste in your phone, send, then ✅ I sent it). When they pick a call time, press Next step.
   - 💡 The faster you answer, the more likely you win the job.
12. **📅 Discovery Call Scheduled:** Text them a reminder the day before. Have the call. When you've booked a visit, press Next step.
   - 💡 A reminder text stops people from forgetting the call.
13. **📐 Estimating/Design:** Visit, measure, and plan the design. When your price is ready, press Next step.
   - 💡 Moving the card keeps your Possible money number honest.
14. **📄 Proposal Sent:** Send the price. Check in after 2 days to answer questions. When they sign, press Next step.
   - 💡 Most people need one friendly check-in before they say yes.
15. **🎉 Closed-Won:** Collect the deposit, sign the contract, and put the job on your calendar. 🎉
   - 💡 Your Jobs won number goes up so you can see what's working.

## ⏰ When someone doesn't answer

16. 4 days after the First text, the ⏰ Check-in text shows up on **🔔 To-Do**. Copy it, send it, press **✅ I sent it**.
   - 💡 A short, friendly nudge often brings back quiet customers.
17. If they answer and book a call, press **📅 They booked a call**.
   - 💡 That moves them to 📅 Discovery Call Scheduled for you.
18. If they say "not this year", open them and press **🔄 Not ready yet**.
   - 💡 They go on the monthly email list so you stay in touch without extra work.

## 📧 Once a month

19. On **🔔 To-Do**, open each email that says **send today**. Copy the subject and the email, send it, press **✅ I sent it**.
   - 💡 3 helpful emails keep you top of mind until they're ready.
20. The first email has blanks like $[amount]. Fill in your real prices before sending.
   - 💡 Real numbers build trust.
21. When a not-ready customer says they're ready, open them and press **They're ready now ➡**.
   - 💡 That moves them to ✅ Pre-Qualified so you can text them.

## 📈 Every Friday: get more leads (10 minutes)

22. Look at **Possible money** and **Jobs won** at the top of My Leads.
   - 💡 It shows if your ads are paying off.
23. Open every card that hasn't moved this week and do its next step, or press **🔄 Not ready yet**.
   - 💡 Stuck cards are lost money. Keep the board clean.
24. Keep your Facebook lead ad running in a 15 to 25 mile circle around your area. See **📘 Setup Help**.
   - 💡 More ads in the right area means more leads to add.
25. Keep the 3 questions on your Facebook form: owns the home, budget, start date.
   - 💡 Those answers let Remod Flow sort every lead for you.
26. When you're ready, set up GoHighLevel using **📘 Setup Help** so the First text goes out by itself.
   - 💡 Then new leads get a text in 1 minute, even while you're on a job site.

## 🎬 Learning with training videos

27. Click **🎬 Training Videos** in the top menu and press play on any video.
   - 💡 Watch a video before you try something new. If a video has a 📄 Printable steps button, print it and follow along.
28. To keep a new video, press **➕ Add a video**, pick it from your computer, and give it a name.
   - 💡 Record yourself doing a task once, and a helper can learn it without you.
29. Use **✏️ Rename** to change a video's name or description, and **🗑 Remove** to delete one.
   - 💡 Clear names make the right video easy to find.

## 🎨 Make it yours

30. On **🏠 My Leads**, press **🎨 Customize boxes** to move, rename or hide boxes. Press **✔ Done** when finished.
   - 💡 This guide updates itself to match your box names and order.
31. Not sure what a button does? Point your mouse at it (or tap it) to see a tip.
   - 💡 Every button has one.

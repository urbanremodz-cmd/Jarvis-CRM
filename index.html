Facebook (Meta): catch new customers automatically

  1. Make the ad. Click Create Campaign, choose Leads, then pick Instant Forms. People fill in the form right inside Facebook.
  2. Pick your area. Under location, choose "People living in this location." Drop a pin on your main Colorado service area and set a 15 to 25 mile circle.
  3. Add three questions. In the form builder, open Custom questions and add these as multiple-choice:
    • Do you own the home being remodeled? (Yes / No)
    • What is your anticipated project budget? (Under $20k / $20k - $40k / $40k+)
    • Target start date? (1-3 Months / 3-6 Months / Researching)
  4. Send them to your video. On the form's Completion Page, paste your GoHighLevel landing page link into the website box. People see your pricing video as soon as they finish.

Until this is set up, copy each lead from the Facebook email and paste it on the Add a Lead page.

GoHighLevel: text and sort customers automatically

  1. Start a workflow. Go to Automation > Workflows and click Create New Workflow.
  2. Say when it starts. Set the trigger to Facebook Lead Form Submitted and pick your Facebook form.
  3. Add the sorter. Add an If/Else step under the trigger. Rule: Property Owner = Yes AND Budget = $20k - $40k or $40k+.
  4. For good customers (the "Yes" side):
    • Internal Notification: text yourself the customer's info.
    • Wait: 1 minute, so the text feels personal.
    • Send SMS: paste the first text (Protocol A) with your booking link.
  5. For not-ready customers (the "No" side):
    • Update Opportunity: move them to Nurture/Follow-Up.
    • Add to Campaign: add them to your 6-month email series.

This app doesn't send texts or emails by itself. It writes them so you can copy them. Once GoHighLevel is set up, it sends them for you.
